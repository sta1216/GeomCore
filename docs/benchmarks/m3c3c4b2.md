# M3c-3c-4b2 benchmark note

## Scope

M3c-3c-4b2 enables whole-cone operands in `boolean_solid_curved` for plane cutters.
It adds no benchmark executable and no timing gate: the acceptance oracles are the
analytic volumes, the single closed shell, outward orientation, bitwise determinism,
and the exact refusal messages. This note records only the numbers measured for this
milestone. Background and the full contract are in ADR-0035.

## Environment

- MSVC 19.44 (Visual Studio 17 2022), Windows x64.
- CMake 4.4.4; `GEOM_WERROR=ON`, `/W4 /WX /utf-8`; zero warnings in Debug and Release.
- Cone fixture tolerance `1e-9`; boolean options tolerance `1e-9`.
- No timing gate applies; no timing is claimed here.

## Fixture

Canonical cone at the origin: `make_cone({0,0,0}, {0,0,1}, 1, 2, 1e-9)`. A plane
perpendicular to the axis at `z = 1` cuts it into an upper small cone (base radius
`0.5`, height `1`, apex present) and a lower frustum. The cutter is the box
`{-2,-2,1}..{2,2,4}`; the union uses the low box `{-2,-2,1}..{2,2,3}`.

The cut rim is the exact analytic circle from `split_solid_face_circle`
(`rho = radius - t*tan(half_angle)`), so the accepted volumes match the closed forms
to `1e-12` absolute; the ordinary curved-boolean corpus is gated at `1e-6` relative.
That extra tightness is the point of the cone fixture: a chordal rim would be far
outside it.

## QA-22

`QA-22: cone/plane booleans are closed and deterministic`
(`tests/unit/brep_boolean_curved_test.cpp`) runs the three operations once each
through the existing `check_cone_boolean` helper. It asserts one shell,
`shell_is_closed_by_uses`, `validate_topology(...).ok()`, a positive (outward)
volume, the analytic volume to `1e-12`, and a bitwise-identical repeat.

## Refusals pinned

| input | result |
| --- | --- |
| cone/cone, cone/torus, cone/cylinder, cone/sphere | `boolean_solid_curved: cone operands only support plane cutters` |
| oblique or axis-containing plane reaching the cone's bounding sphere | `boolean_solid_curved: cone cutters must be perpendicular to the cone axis; oblique and axis-containing planes are deferred to M3c-3c-4b3` |
| trimmed cone fragment (not one full-chart loop) | `boolean_solid_curved: cone operands must be whole cones; trimmed cone fragments are not supported` |
| cone placement where both seam copies of a cut-circle point round to the same double (base centre `y = +-2`, radius `0.5`) | `boolean_solid_curved: cone seam copies coincide at this placement; cone booleans at this offset are deferred to M3c-3c-4b3` |

## Deferred

Trimmed cone tessellation stays in M3c-3c-4b3: `tessellate_cone_face` fans the whole
base ring to one apex vertex, which is not the sampling of a split fragment. Oblique
and axis-containing planes, cone/cone and other curved/curved cone cutters, and
trimmed cone fragments also stay deferred; they have no analytic cut rim or chart
contract in this slice. Re-centring translated cones so their seam copies stay
distinct is also deferred: the current slice refuses those placements deterministically
instead of repairing the duplicate rim.
